## Verified gameplay journey and useful exact codes

1. **Prepare first, then hunt.** Any knife, skinning hook and butchering table are upstream baseline equipment. Stone-age route exists: knap `butchering:hook-flint` from `game:flint` (recipe yields two hooks); grid hook `butchering:butcherhook-flint-north` uses two hooks, logs, sticks, flint and resin (fat/rendered-fat alternatives), NOT rope. `butchering:butchertable-primitive-north` uses logs, a stone knife, a stone axe, flint and stones; knife/axe ingredients are not marked `isTool` in this recipe, so do not promise they survive crafting. Prefer these over mandatory iron/saw stations. Upstream table is a backpack-slot item, placed like a filled skep with Ctrl + slot 1–4/scrollwheel, three blocks wide extending two to the right; reserve the footprint.
2. **Corpse transport or emergency field harvest.** Right-click a dead, unharvested supported creature with an empty active/right hand. Large corpses need an open backpack slot unless an appropriate Butchering bag allows inventory storage. Upstream explicitly permits small chickens, hares and foxes in ordinary inventory slots. Corpses slow movement and spoil. Do not prescribe Carry On as mandatory: native corpse pickup is in Butchering itself. Field cutting remains the emergency lower-yield option, not the recommended tutorial route.
3. **Skin on the hook.** Place the corpse by right-click; hold right-click on the hook with a knife until skinned. Implementation requires `ItemKnife`; cleaver is NOT a hook substitute. Hook processing drops hide/fur/feather-like skinning-exclusive resources and changes corpse state `dead` → `skinned` (see hook `processItem`, lines 248–269). Exact representative asset-expanded corpse progression is `butchering:deadpig-male-eurasian-normal-1-dead` → `butchering:deadpig-male-eurasian-normal-1-skinned` → `butchering:deadpig-male-eurasian-normal-1-bledout`; pig variants include type/skin/size/texture/state, not one universal `butchering:carcass` ID. These are source-expanded identifiers, not live-registration verification.
4. **Wait for bloodletting AFTER skinning.** The `skinned` corpse cannot be removed from the hook (`TryTake`, lines 119–136). Hook tick checks elapsed game hours since skinning: strictly >1 small / >2 medium / >4 large workload before `bledout` (lines 62–72); this is not a fixed real-time timer. Then take it down empty-handed. Upstream says body turns less red. **No distinct “dressed” corpse state was found:** do not invent a dressing station/state or a separate evisceration action between skinning and bleeding.
5. **Optional blood capture.** Put an empty bucket directly beneath before bleeding completes; output `butchering:bloodportion` (or animal's configured blood type). Upstream guide recommends 2–4 blocks below; DLL actually searches aligned bucket block entities 1–6 blocks down (`SwitchToBledOut`, lines 139–203), accepting empty or same-blood content. Use the conservative 2–4 recommendation; do not claim any arbitrary liquid container works. Collection is not required for corpse state advancement.
6. **Dress/process on table.** Place the bled-out corpse on table; hold right-click with knife or cleaver. Table declares processing state `bledout`; a cleaver is optional and uses less tool durability. Table process combines Butchering + native harvestable drops while excluding skinning-exclusive drops, then consumes the corpse and may place the configured `AnimalCarcass` bone carcass. Upstream says break that carcass for bones; do not require an invented universal carcass item or promise every species has identical drops.
7. **Use the branches rather than collecting an arbitrary shopping list.** Representative outputs are `butchering:offal-bloody`, `butchering:primemeat-raw`, `butchering:sinew-wet`, `butchering:strongbone`, plus native hide/meat/bone resources. Yield is species/condition dependent. Native info says clean offal in a barrel, dry wet sinew before bow/sewing/glue use, and smoking uses a rack with firepit below. Keep meat preservation, blood cookery, bone tools, bags and healing meat optional follow-ups. Bone pickaxe is disabled; class-gated recipes need alternatives. Pile-curing behavior is a separate compatibility issue, not a prerequisite for this quest pack.

## Leather / cross-mod boundary

Enabled local manifest includes `tailorsdelight` 2.2.2, `immersivewoodworking` 1.3.13, `expandedfoods` 2.0.0-dev.17, `efchefstricks` 1.0.0-dev.6, `em` 3.8.3 and `aculinaryartillery` 2.0.0-dev.29. Skinning is a source of raw hides, **not finished leather**.

- Exact pack config `config/ModConfig/ImmersiveWoodworking/general.json`: `EnableBarkChain=true`, `DisableVanillaLogTannin=true`. Do not teach vanilla oak-log-in-barrel tannin as the pack route. Primary `mods/immersivewoodworking_1.3.13.zip!/assets/immersivewoodworking/recipes/barrel/weaktannin-bark.json` and `strongtannin-bark.json`: tag `bark-tannin` + 1 L water → 1 L weak tannin; tag `bark-tannin` + 1 L weak tannin → 1 L strong tannin, 72 seal-hours; dry-tag variants 48 hours; crushed-tag variants use 2 L input/output and 24 hours. Codes `game:weaktanninportion`, `game:strongtanninportion`. This is the verified bark-to-tannin bridge; this audit did not independently inspect the base-game hide-soaking/scraping/tanning barrel assets, so do not add exact base hide-stage amounts/times from memory.
- Primary `mods/tailors_delight-2.2.2.zip!/assets/tailorsdelight/recipes/grid/hidebundle.json`: size-tagged hides + stick + knife tool + awl tool can make `tailorsdelight:leatherbundle-raw`/`-pelt`; e.g. three small raw hides or one medium raw hide per raw bundle. `recipes/barrel/soaking_bundles.json`: one raw bundle + 4 L diluted borax for 24 hours OR limewater for 28 hours → `game:hide-soaked-medium`. These are optional aggregation routes, not a new required butcher station.
- `...!/assets/tailorsdelight/recipes/grid/leatherbundle_cutting.json`: two plain leather bundles + generic knife → three `game:leather-normal-plain`; clothier alternative uses one bundle + ruler + shears → two. Do not use the clothier route as universal progression. Raw-bundle cutting similarly targets small raw hides.
- `...!/assets/tailorsdelight/patches/vs-recipepatches/survival-recipes-grid-armor-leather.json` adds awl/leather strips to leather armor recipes. Link the native finished-good recipe rather than copying vanilla requirements.
- Optional oiled-hide route exists in `mods/ExpandedFoods_ChefsTricks_1.0.0-dev.6.zip!/assets/efchefstricks/recipes/barrel/oiledhide.json`: raw hide + lard or supported food-oil portions → oiled hide, 12 seal-hours, liquid amount varies by size. **Oiled hide is not proof of tanned leather.** Keep it optional, not the leather completion criterion.

## Recommended safe quest spine

Prepare knife + primitive hook + primitive table → choose native corpse transport over emergency field cutting → retrieve one supported corpse → knife-skin on hook → leave it until bled out (optional blood bucket) → process bled-out corpse on table → preserve/use an actually obtained product → optional raw-hide/leather cross-guide and better bags/tools.

Use manual/read acknowledgements for native actions unless the parent framework already has proven events for these exact operations. Inventory possession is evidence of possession, **not evidence the player hunted, skinned, bled, dressed or tanned it personally**. Do not mint action completion from an item detector. Keep exact species-specific corpse IDs as examples or dynamically validated alternatives rather than requiring a random animal/skin. Do not gate the main line on rare primemeat, sinew, ivory, blood cooking, a class trait, an optional compat provider, or disabled tools. Native recipe links own variants and precise crafting quantities.
